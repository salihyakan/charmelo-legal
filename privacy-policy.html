Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Wonderkin ("the App", "we", "us"), developed by Salih Yakan (an individual developer), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App on iOS or Android. Contact us anytime at salihyakann@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Wonderkin does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or photos.

  • Anonymous device identifier. On first launch, the App creates a random, anonymous player ID (via Firebase Anonymous Authentication) and a random display name (which you can change). Neither is linked to your real-world identity.
  • Gameplay data. Your progress (currencies, buildings, collected companions, settings) is stored locally on your device.
  • Leaderboard data. If you take part in the daily event leaderboard, your anonymous player ID, display name, and score are stored on our server (Google Firestore) and are readable by other players of the App. This data resets/rotates daily.
  • Advertising identifiers (future). Wonderkin does not currently show ads. If/when an advertising SDK (e.g. Google AdMob) is integrated, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) may be used to deliver ads — personalized ads only if you have opted in via the in-app consent screen (Settings → Privacy), and iOS additionally requires your separate App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized/contextual ads may be shown without this consent.
  • In-app purchases. Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Standard technical data. Like most apps, basic technical/diagnostic data (app version, crash logs, device type) may be collected by our infrastructure providers to keep the App working.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and restore your gameplay progress.
  • To operate the daily leaderboard/event feature.
  • To show ads (once integrated) and process purchases.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the App.

We do not sell your information.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — stores the anonymous player ID, display name, and leaderboard scores. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — process in-app purchases under their own privacy policies.
  • Google AdMob (future, once integrated) — will deliver ads under Google's Privacy Policy and, where required, Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow.

4. Children's Privacy

Wonderkin is a general-audience casual game and is not intentionally directed at collecting personal information from children. On first launch, players are asked a simple age question. If a player indicates they are under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in their country), the App disables personalized ads, tracking-based advertising, and the App Tracking Transparency prompt for that install. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the anonymous, non-identifying technical data described above, which is necessary for basic app functionality. A parent or guardian who believes their child has provided us with personal information may contact us at salihyakann@gmail.com to request its deletion.

5. Your Rights (EEA/UK/GDPR and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, object to or restrict our processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Because Wonderkin does not collect directly identifying information, most of these rights can be exercised simply by:

  • Uninstalling the App (deletes all locally stored save data), and/or
  • Reopening Settings → Privacy in the App at any time to change or withdraw your ad-personalization consent, and/or
  • Contacting salihyakann@gmail.com with your in-app display name and approximate play dates so we can locate and delete your leaderboard entry.

6. Data Retention

Local save data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Leaderboard entries are tied to a daily event period and are superseded each day; historical entries are periodically purged.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures (including Firebase's built-in access rules, see our Firestore security rules) to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will bump the version number above and, where required, prompt you to re-consent inside the App.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email salihyakann@gmail.com.

  • Oyun verisi. İlerlemeniz (para birimleri, binalar, toplanan dostlar, ayarlar) cihazınızda yerel olarak saklanır.
  • Sıralama (leaderboard) verisi. Günlük etkinlik sıralamasına katılırsanız; anonim oyuncu kimliğiniz, görünen adınız ve puanınız sunucumuzda (Google Firestore) saklanır ve Uygulamanın diğer oyuncuları tarafından görüntülenebilir. Bu veri her gün yenilenir.
  • Reklam kimlikleri (ileride). Wonderkin şu anda reklam göstermemektedir. Bir reklam SDK'sı (ör. Google AdMob) entegre edildiğinde, cihazınızın reklam kimliği (iOS'ta IDFA, Android'de Advertising ID) reklam sunmak için kullanılabilir — kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar yalnızca uygulama içi onay ekranından (Ayarlar → Gizlilik) izin verdiyseniz gösterilir; iOS'ta ayrıca ayrı bir App Tracking Transparency izni de gerekir. Bu onay olmadan kişiselleştirilmemiş/bağlamsal reklamlar gösterilebilir.

  • Standart teknik veri. Çoğu uygulama gibi, Uygulamanın çalışır durumda kalması için altyapı sağlayıcılarımız temel teknik/tanılama verisi (uygulama sürümü, çökme kayıtları, cihaz tipi) toplayabilir.

2. Bilgileri Nasıl Kullanıyoruz

  • Günlük sıralama/etkinlik özelliğini işletmek için.
  • Reklam göstermek (entegre edildiğinde) ve satın almaları işlemek için.
  • Çökmeleri teşhis etmek ve Uygulamayı geliştirmek için.

Bilgilerinizi satmıyoruz.
